## Special personal information and children

No field is expressly designed to collect POPIA special personal information or children's data. Free-text transaction descriptions, notes, category names, goal names, and household labels could nevertheless contain sensitive or third-party information. No age gate or parental-consent process was found. This is a **POSSIBLE** risk requiring audience and product decisions (`COMP-009`).

## Data minimisation observations

- Raw CSV content is processed locally; only selected structured records are submitted.
- Shared reports default to aggregates and omit transaction details, notes and goals.
- Shared template amounts are excluded unless explicitly enabled.
- Email check-ins default to privacy-minimised content.
- Demo sample transactions are never transferred into an account.
- Free-text fields and immutable snapshots increase residual sensitivity and require clear notices and retention decisions.

## Export coverage

The versioned portable backup includes domain data needed for restoration and deliberately excludes authentication data, provider credentials and notification-delivery logs (`src/shared/utilities/portableBackup.ts`, `supabase/migrations/202610020001_add_backup_restore.sql`). A portability export is not a full infrastructure backup and may not satisfy every access request without operational review. The format-by-format coverage and exclusions are recorded in [Data access, export, and correction](data-access-and-correction.md).
