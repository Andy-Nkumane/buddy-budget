# Operator and legal governance checklist

No item is complete merely because this checklist exists. Retain dated, secret-free evidence privately and record only safe status summaries in the repository.

## P0 — before inviting authorised testers

- [ ] Identify and legally verify the responsible party/private body, head, legal form, jurisdiction, physical/service address and principal place of business.
- [ ] Identify and register the Information Officer; designate/register Deputies where justified; retain the Regulator reference privately.
- [ ] Establish monitored privacy, PAIA, incident and complaints channels with owners and alternates.
- [ ] Complete the [processor register](processor-register.md) for the actual Supabase, GitHub and Mailjet accounts.
- [ ] Obtain/review accepted written operator terms against POPIA section 21; record gaps and approved exceptions.
- [ ] Complete and approve a section 72 assessment for every foreign-country flow and onward transfer.
- [ ] Verify provider regions for primary data, backups, logs, support and subprocessors from dashboards/contracts—not `.env` URLs or assumptions.
- [ ] Complete incident contacts/provider escalation and the synthetic tabletop.
- [ ] Keep tester signup restricted by the deployed Auth allowlist or disable public signup.

## P1 — PAIA, transparency and operating records

- [ ] Obtain legal confirmation of PAIA private-body status; do not rely on the expired general exemption.
- [ ] Prepare, approve and publish the section 51 manual using the current Regulator template and verified operator facts.
- [ ] Implement the current prescribed request, fee, outcome, third-party, refusal and complaint procedure.
- [ ] Maintain a restricted request register and collect the fields required for current PAIA annual reporting.
- [ ] Reconcile the public Privacy Policy, Terms and Contact pages with the approved operator identity, provider register, transfer assessment and request channels.
- [ ] Approve provider/subprocessor review cadence and change-notification ownership.
- [ ] Approve record, provider-log, backup, support-case, request and contract retention/exit rules.

## Conditional ECTA and consumer governance

Buddy Budget is currently positioned as private, experimental and non-commercial. If it offers goods or services for sale, hire or exchange through an electronic transaction, obtain legal review of ECTA section 43 before launch. Verify and disclose applicable supplier facts rather than copying placeholders:

- full name/legal status, physical/service address, telephone, website and email;
- registration, office-bearer and place-of-registration information where applicable;
- memberships/codes, service characteristics, total price and payment terms;
- agreement, record access, delivery/performance, return/refund, dispute-resolution and privacy/security information; and
- applicable cooling-off and consumer rights.

Reassess CPA and related duties at the same gate. “Free,” “experimental,” or “at your own risk” is not a substitute for a factual applicability assessment.

## Review triggers

Repeat this governance review before changing provider, region, hosting, email delivery, public audience, pricing, analytics, support tooling, bank integration, payment/credit/advice scope, data categories, retention, or legal operator. Also reassess after an incident, provider acquisition/contract change, subprocessor notice, or material regulator guidance change.
