# Processor and third-party register

Reviewed on 18 October 2026. This register separates repository evidence, provider-published information, and owner-dependent deployment facts. It is not proof that a contract was accepted, a particular account uses a stated plan or region, or a provider has been legally classified correctly for every processing activity.

## Role definitions used for review

- **Responsible party:** determines the purpose of and means for processing personal information.
- **Operator:** processes personal information for a responsible party under a contract or mandate without coming under the responsible party's direct authority.
- **Independent recipient/responsible party:** receives information for its own independently determined purpose rather than only acting on Buddy Budget instructions.

POPIA section 21 requires a written contract between a responsible party and an operator that requires the operator to establish and maintain the section 19 security measures. Legal review must confirm each role against the actual agreement and activity; a product label such as “processor” does not decide the South African classification.

Client-side npm libraries are delivered as bundled code and no analytics SDK or direct third-party runtime endpoint was found. They are supply-chain dependencies, not automatically personal-information recipients. Reassess if telemetry, CDN-hosted scripts, bank feeds, crash reporting, support tooling, payment services, or new email providers are added.

## Mandatory evidence record

For every active provider, the owner must retain privately:

1. contracting legal entity, service/account owner, plan and effective agreement/DPA version;
2. purpose, instructions, data categories, people, systems and minimum access;
3. primary, backup, log, support and remote-access locations;
4. POPIA role analysis, section 21 written terms, confidentiality and security measures;
5. section 72 transfer ground and onward-transfer controls;
6. subprocessors, objection/change-notice mechanism and review date;
7. retention, return/deletion, backup expiry, legal holds and account termination;
8. incident-notification timing/channel and cooperation duties;
9. access/correction/deletion/export assistance and response owner; and
10. approval, exceptions, renewal date and exit/portability plan.

Public provider pages are supporting evidence only and may change. Capture the reviewed version/date without copying credentials or private contract terms into the public repository.
