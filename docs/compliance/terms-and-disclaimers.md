# Terms, disclaimer and contact applicability

Reviewed on 16 October 2026. This is a technical applicability record, not legal advice or approval to publish binding terms.

## Confirmed product facts

- Buddy Budget is positioned as experimental, non-commercial, private and limited to authorised testing.
- Public landing, registration, calculator, demo, report and template routes remain present in the source.
- The application performs budgeting calculations, forecasts and deterministic recommendations, but no reviewed feature selects, recommends or transacts in a financial product.
- No verified legal operator identity, service address, support address, complaints channel, governing-law decision or legal approval exists in the repository.

## ECTA assessment

ECTA applies generally to electronic communications and data messages. Its supplier and consumer-transaction provisions are conditional on the actual online offering and transaction. Section 43 identifies supplier information required where goods or services are offered for sale, hire or exchange through an electronic transaction. The repository cannot supply the required operator details without owner evidence.

The Terms and Contact routes therefore disclose the gap and remain draft-blocked. They do not assert that the authorised-testing label prevents ECTA from applying, and they do not invent the missing supplier information.

## CPA assessment

CPA applicability remains conditional on the actual supply and whether it occurs in the ordinary course of business. Public acquisition routes make reliance on a non-commercial label alone unsafe. The draft Terms preserve non-waivable rights and avoid a blanket liability waiver. Before public, promoted or commercial supply, the owner must obtain legal review and implement fair terms, conspicuous risk notices, supplier information, complaints and redress appropriate to the operating facts.

## Financial-information boundary

The current product is evidenced as a budgeting and record-keeping tool, not a provider of personalised financial-product advice or intermediary execution. The disclaimer tells testers to verify calculations and obtain qualified advice where needed. It also states that disclaimer wording cannot change the legal character of future regulated conduct.

## Publication blockers

- Verified responsible party/operator legal identity and legal status.
- Physical and service addresses and monitored electronic/telephone contacts where required.
- Eligible tester and minimum-age decision.
- Approved complaints and escalation process.
- Governing-law, forum and dispute-process decision.
- Legal approval, version and effective date.
- Actual access restriction consistent with the authorised-testing claim.
